## Product direction

Keep Home, Practice and Library as the three primary destinations. Home answers “What should I study next?”, Practice answers “How do I want to train?”, and Library answers “What does this word mean and how do I use it?”. Keep Progress & backup one step from Home and Grammar inside Library. Prioritize the daily lesson, due review and word lookup before adding new modes.

### Implementation map

- [index.html](index.html) combines the app shell, layered CSS, five inline scripts, routing, course data, lesson/review state, progress and backup. Several flows also have later overrides.
- [vocabulary-general-ui.js](vocabulary-general-ui.js) owns curated review intervals, typing/listening, article practice and general study behaviors. [vocabulary-unified-library.js](vocabulary-unified-library.js) builds the unified Library, filters, shared detail and collection routing around stable IDs.
- [vocabulary-latest-database.js](vocabulary-latest-database.js) is the 5.36 MB authoritative browser export. The curated, pilot, frequency and gender-index assets also load in the shell. [vocabulary-manifest.json](vocabulary-manifest.json) gives the dataset version, hashes and counts. Its 2,620 noun-class entries, 2,599 article-bank entries and 2,444 fixed-article practice items have different denominators. Older README counts reflect an earlier V11 dataset.
- [sw.js](sw.js) precaches the versioned study export alongside the shell. Older vocabulary assets and the manifest use a network-first path. Keep export, UI and cache revisions coherent. The local upgrade reproduced a stale root document under the new cache key. Revalidating HTML on the temporary server fixed the reproduction. [scripts/check-study-integration.cjs](scripts/check-study-integration.cjs) and [scripts/check-vocabulary-sanity.js](scripts/check-vocabulary-sanity.js) cover structural regression.

### Visual and responsive observations

The ivory, pine and sage palette already gives Verbwerk a recognizable identity. Home gives the strongest surface to promotional copy ahead of the action. Practice nests four large cards inside another card. Library gives rows detail-page density by putting examples and two audio controls on many results. Establish one primary action, quieter secondary actions and contextual links. Use consistent states and icons across the three views.

The three landing pages had no horizontal overflow at 320, 390, 768 and 1440 CSS px. At 320 px the two-column Practice grid produces tiles up to 163 px tall and Library categories wrap. Existing rules stack Library fields at 560 px, but there is no broader desktop shell. Tune short mobile viewports first. On wide screens, put secondary context next to the main flow and keep study text in a readable column.

## German learning components

| Component | Current behavior | Proposed behavior and acceptance |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Article and noun forms** | Article text appears in headings, rows and feedback. Detail exposes plurals and number notes. The integrated quiz uses reviewed fixed articles. | Put a written article chip before the noun and a compact **Plural** line below its meaning. Use the same gender colors across views. Keep variants and articles attached to their senses. Show the full plural, including the plural article when useful, and distinguish “usually singular”, “plural only” and “plural unavailable”. Reveal the correct article with written feedback after submission. |
| **Compounds** | No dedicated breakdown on the checked detail paths. | Add an optional reviewed **Compound parts** section below noun forms. For **die Datenbank**, show **Daten** (“data”) + **Bank** (“bank”), with **Bank** labeled as the head. Link verified component senses and show linking elements where supplied. Omit the section until a decomposition has been reviewed. |
| **Examples and grammar** | Bilingual examples, clickable semantic glosses and coverage labels exist. The manifest lists 1,763 trusted and 3,633 candidate bilingual examples. | Put one trusted German sentence directly below meaning/forms and its English translation below it. In recall, let the learner reveal the translation. Keep the inflected token in the sentence and expose its lemma and gloss on tap or keyboard. Label candidate text, preserve provenance, and use only reviewed material in scored prompts. |
| **Verb and word-class forms** | Full course conjugations and patterns, variable depth for expanded verbs and word-class grammar from the export. | Give verbs a short construction line such as **teilnehmen an + Dativ**. Put separability, auxiliary and past forms under **Forms**. Use the same slots for adjective degrees, preposition case and connector word order when available. |
| **Pronunciation** | Browser speech synthesis at 1× and 0.7×. Many rows show two large controls. | Place a named **Listen** action beside the lemma, with a slow option in detail and listening practice. Surface ready, playing and unavailable states. Request a German voice where available, cancel previous playback, and offer retry or skip when audio fails. The listening prompt's accessible label must preserve the answer. |
| **Recall and scheduling** | General practice schedules correct answers after 1/3/7/14/30 days and a miss after ten minutes. Verb and article review have separate prioritization. | Use one explicit **Show answer** action for self-check cards and a 150–180 ms reveal with an immediate swap under reduced motion. Keep test answers hidden through submission. Show “6 due”, “2 of 8” and “Next review tomorrow” from real state. Distinguish Introduced, Learning and Mastered. One correct answer begins review. |

Article chips use foreground/background pairs. **der** light #205078/#E4EFFA, dark #BDDFFF/#243B51. **die** light #713D62/#F5E7F0, dark #F5CEE6/#493142. **das** light #59447D/#EEE8F8, dark #E2D4FF/#3D3251. Keep the article visible in text and use a neutral “Plural” label to distinguish plural **die** from feminine singular. Verify computed contrast in all states. Use WCAG 2.2 AA as the baseline, including 4.5:1 for normal text and reflow at 320 CSS px. Aim for 44 × 44 CSS px on common controls and 48 px for answer choices. [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/)

- **Typography.** Instrument Sans as a proposed self-hosted variable font with system sans fallback. Verify offline loading and support for ä, ö, ü, ß and ẞ. Body 16/24, helper 14/20, label 13/18, section title 20/28, page title 28/34 and study word 32/38. Use tabular numerals for progress.
- **Spacing.** 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32 and 48 px. Use 16 px mobile gutters, 24 px tablet gutters, 16–24 px card padding, 12 px control radius and 16 px card radius.
- **Responsive.** Below 480 px stack actions where labels wrap and use compact single-column rows. From 480–767 px allow side-by-side actions when they fit. From 768 px pair Home’s next action with a short progress panel. From 1024 px use contained header navigation, a 1120 px content maximum and a roughly 680 px reading column. Library can use list plus detail with one active detail route and a single-column fallback.
- **Interaction.** Use native links for navigable details and buttons for actions. Give each view a named heading. Mark German phrases inside the English shell. Preserve visible focus across themes, polite feedback and focus restoration. Account for safe areas, the on-screen keyboard, zoom and long compounds. Pair color with text for selected, correct, incorrect and disabled states.
