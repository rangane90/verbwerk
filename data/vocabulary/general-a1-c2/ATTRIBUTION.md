## Supplemental records

The source-packs/verbwerk_wikidata_supplement_v1/ pack adds 24 German entries missing from the pinned source snapshot. It uses linked German Wikidata Lexemes (CC0 1.0); the pack preserves each Lexeme ID and its source record. Its new German learner definitions and example sentences are Verbwerk learning content released under CC BY 4.0. Existing Verbwerk meanings retain their original project terms. No CEFR labels or category tags were added to these records.
